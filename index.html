At work

For our customers, that started around the coffee machine: food, coffee, and a place to meet. For our employees, it meant the culture we built together. For me, it meant finding my own way to be an entrepreneur.

Coaching leaders

Coaching is another way to explore that original question with someone else, alongside building and, now, writing.

Coaching

Are you satisfied with your life right now?

At work? In life?

You can carry a lot of responsibility and still need space to work out what you want.

One-to-one coaching for founders and leaders.

Trained with Jean-Daniel Rémond · Founders coached from seed to Series B

Book a conversation ↗

Zoom in

What needs your attention now?

We start with what brings you here and create space to think. What’s happening? What do you need? What could you do next?

Some conversations I have:

  • A co-founder whose company makes 100 million in revenue, but who is unhappy with their life.
  • A co-founder who wants to leave after ten years, but doesn’t know how.
  • A leader who feels close to burnout.

We look at what you want, what you feel obliged to do, and what makes it difficult to choose—including the expectations you carry from your family, your peers, or yourself.

Zoom out

What kind of life do you want?

Work can take up much of our lives while drawing on only part of who we are. We step back from the immediate situation to look at the wider picture.

In coaching, I look at six dimensions of energy: physical, emotional, mental, social, spiritual, and creative. What gives you energy? What drains it? What have you left little room for?

Then we bring it back to life: a choice, a conversation, a boundary, or something to try. The aim is to make room for the parts of yourself you want to develop.

What I bring

I spent eight years building TOTEM. I know the founder’s seat: hiring, fundraising, carrying responsibility, and making decisions when there is no comfortable option.

I also know what it is like to need support. Being coached through difficult periods changed how I approached my work and my own choices. That experience is part of why I do this.
