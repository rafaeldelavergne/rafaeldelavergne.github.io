##inherited

Some of what I’ve inherited is a gift. Some of it I want to question.

From my parents, from the world around me. I’m trying to understand what I choose to keep, and what I want to change. Your turn — a few things people often carry:

  • Work hard, complain little.
  • Be reasonable.
  • A stable job is a safe life.
  • Don’t take up too much space.
  • Rest is something you earn.

##three stages of a life

1 · Adapting

Growing up, we build adaptation software: to our parents, our siblings, our friends, society. Like a sponge, the brain optimises other people’s energy. Each of us takes the shape our neighbours leave us.

2 · Becoming yourself

Often after a shock, you rewrite what is good and bad for you, across all six dimensions. For me that meant entrepreneurship, philosophy, coaching, neuroscience, wisdom traditions — even shamanism. You become yourself, but mostly among your own.

“And those who were dancing were thought to be insane by those who could not hear the music.”

— often attributed to Friedrich Nietzsche

3 · Yourself, among everyone

Recharge alone or with your own, then go back out to create and change things. Back and forth, like riding a bicycle. The goal: a wanderer who can go anywhere and remain yourself.

##energy

Everything is energy.

In coaching, I look at six dimensions of it: physical, emotional, mental, aesthetic, spiritual, social.

Many of us live on two.

Usually the mind and the work. They run full, and the other four wait quietly. Nothing is wrong with you: it is just where the energy has been going.

The others are room to grow.

You don’t need to push harder on what is already full. A small step on a forgotten dimension changes the whole shape, and you feel it.

Where are you today?

Each one can be cultivated, a little at a time. Tap a dimension to raise or lower it.
