A question I keep coming back to

What does it mean
to be yourself?

Am I living the life I want, or running someone else’s program?

Some of what I’ve inherited from my parents and the world around me is a gift. Some of it I want to question. I’m trying to understand what I choose to keep, and what I want to change.

Where it takes me

This question has led me into philosophy, shamanism, and a lot of questions about my own life. I explore these things for myself first. I’m still figuring out what to make of them.

About

Eight years

TOTEM

Co-founder & CEO

I co-founded TOTEM in 2017, bringing food, coffee, and shared spaces into workplaces. I built a team of around forty people and worked across product, operations, hiring, and fundraising.

During Covid, revenue fell by 95%. Restructuring the company meant making difficult decisions with consequences for people I knew well.

Being coached through that period changed how I approached responsibility and the choices I was making for myself.

Since then

Working with leaders

I’ve coached founders from seed to Series B and trained with Jean-Daniel Rémond. My interest has widened beyond running a business to the person behind the role: what they want, what they believe, and how they choose to live.

Before TOTEM

Earlier experiences

I served as a lieutenant in the French Army, worked in startups, and contributed to a team project to build a sailing drone.

I studied at École Polytechnique and UC Berkeley, focusing on entrepreneurship and data.

Outside work

I climb. I like the concentration it asks for, and the process of trying a movement, understanding what went wrong, and trying again.

Coaching

One-to-one conversations for leaders.

You might be facing a difficult decision, stepping into a new role, or realizing that the life you’ve built no longer fits.

The whole person

I’m interested in what gives you energy and what drains it—physically, emotionally, mentally, socially, spiritually, and creatively. We can explore whichever of these matters to the choices you’re facing.
