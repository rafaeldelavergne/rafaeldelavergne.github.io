Coaching leaders

I trained with Jean-Daniel Rémond and have coached founders from seed to Series B. I’m interested in the person carrying the responsibility: what they want, what they believe they have to do, and where those two things pull apart.

Coaching is another way to explore that original question with someone else, alongside building and, now, writing.

Coaching

You can carry a lot of responsibility and still need space to work out what you want.

One-to-one coaching for founders and leaders.

Trained with Jean-Daniel Rémond · Founders coached from seed to Series B

When something needs to change

You might have a decision you keep postponing. A role that takes more from you than you want to give. Or a life that looks right from the outside but no longer feels like yours.

Sometimes the question is about work. Sometimes it is about the expectations you carry into it—from your family, your peers, or yourself.

What we work on

We start with what is happening in your life. We look at what you want, what you feel obliged to do, and what makes it difficult to choose.

The aim is to give you more room to respond deliberately. That might mean having a conversation you’ve avoided, setting a boundary, making a decision, or trying something different and seeing what you learn.

What I bring

I spent eight years building TOTEM. I know the founder’s seat: hiring, fundraising, carrying responsibility, and making decisions when there is no comfortable option.

I also know what it is like to need support. Being coached through difficult periods changed how I approached my work and my own choices. That experience is part of why I do this.

There is more to you than work

We can look at what gives you energy and what drains it: your body, emotions, thoughts, relationships, sense of meaning, and creativity.

These physical, emotional, mental, social, spiritual, and creative dimensions are possible ways into the conversation. We follow what matters to you.
