The short version

I'm Rafaël de Lavergne. I spent eight years building TOTEM as its founder and CEO, growing a team of around forty people and navigating the uncertainty that comes with running a company.

That experience shapes my coaching. I know what it is like to carry responsibility, make decisions without enough information, and wonder whether the thing you are building still fits the person you are becoming.

I've since worked with founders from seed to Series B and trained with Jean-Daniel Rémond. The thread through this work is agency: helping people find their own direction and act on it.

Outside work, I climb. I like the combination of attention, movement, and having to try again.

When we work together

You don't need
a finished question.

You may be facing a decision that won't resolve itself in a spreadsheet. Your company may be changing faster than your role. Or things may look good from the outside, while something no longer feels right.

We start with what is actually happening. We examine the assumptions underneath it, clarify what you want to take responsibility for, and work toward choices you can act on.

The approach

A founder's experience.
A coach's attention.

Eight years as founder and CEO of TOTEM inform my understanding of leadership. Coaching founders from seed to Series B and training with Jean-Daniel Rémond have shaped how I listen and work.

The aim is to strengthen your own judgment and freedom to act.
