Eight years

TOTEM

Co-founder & CEO

I co-founded TOTEM in 2017. We brought food, coffee, and shared spaces into workplaces, with a service combining physical operations and software.

I spent eight years building the company and a team of around forty people. The work ranged from product and operations to hiring, fundraising, and deciding where the business should go next.

Through the difficult periods

During Covid, revenue fell by 95%. I dealt with restructuring and the decisions that come with trying to keep a company going when its market changes overnight.

Being coached during that period shaped my interest in supporting other founders. It also left me with a practical question that I still work on: how do you keep a company’s daily decisions connected to its direction?

Current practice

Coaching

Founders & leaders

I work one-to-one with founders, including leaders of seed to Series B companies. We work on decisions, changes in role, and the relationship between the company they’re building and the life they want.

I trained with Jean-Daniel Rémond. My experience as a founder informs the conversations, without assuming that what worked for me will work for someone else.
